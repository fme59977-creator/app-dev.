# Star Wars

> "A long time ago in a galaxy far, far away..."

## Overview
**Star Wars** is a groundbreaking science fiction saga that explores the battle between the Light and Dark sides of the Force. It spans generations of heroes, villains, and galactic conflict.

## Core Themes
- Hope and redemption
- Power and responsibility
- Legacy and identity

## Key Characters
- Luke Skywalker – Jedi Knight and symbol of hope
- Darth Vader – Fallen Jedi turned Sith Lord
- Leia Organa – Rebel leader and strategist
- Yoda – Ancient master of the Force
